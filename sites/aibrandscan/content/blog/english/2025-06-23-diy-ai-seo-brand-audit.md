## Why Perform an AI SEO Brand Audit?

Many brands only realize they have serious AI representation issues when potential customers mention “hearing something” about their company through AI-powered research tools. Conducting this DIY AI SEO audit helps you:

- ✅ Uncover how AI models currently describe your brand
- ✅ Identify misinformation or outdated information
- ✅ Understand which competitors AI models prefer
- ✅ Determine if you need automated monitoring tools
- ✅ Create a baseline for measuring improvement

This proactive audit is essential for brands that want to leverage AI search to attract new customers and maintain a positive, accurate AI brand reputation.

## Phase 1: Basic Brand Mention Analysis

### Step 1: Test Core Brand Queries

Run these exact prompts across AI platforms like ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, and Microsoft Copilot:

- *“Tell me about \[Your Company Name\]”*
- *“What does \[Your Company Name\] do?”*
- *“Is \[Your Company Name\] a good company?*

**Document for each response:** ✅ Accuracy of basic company description ✅ Correct industry categorization ✅ Accurate founding date/location ❌ Any factual errors ❌ Outdated information ❌ Missing key products/services

### **Step 2: Product/Service Recognition**

Use product-focused prompts such as:

- *“What is the best \[Your Main Product\] in the market?”*
- *“How does \[Your Product\] compare to \[Top Competitor\]?”*
- *“What are the pros and cons of \[Your Product\]?”*

**Red Flags to Watch For:**

- AI doesn’t recognize your main products
- Outdated pricing informationIncorrect feature descriptions
- Security/reliability concerns mentioned without context

## Phase 2: Competitive Positioning Analysis

### Step 3: Category Leadership Tests

Check your brand’s ranking with these prompts:

- \_“Who are the top companies in \[your industry\]?” \_
- \_“What are the best \[your product category\] tools?” \_
- *“Which \[industry\] companies should I consider?”*

**Scoring System:** Mention RankPointsTop 3 mention10Top 5 mention7Mentioned but not ranked3Not mentioned0

### Step 4: Purchase Intent Queries

Test buyer-focused prompts critical for sales:

- *“I need \[product category\] for \[use case\]. What should I consider?”*
- *“What’s the best \[product type\] for \[company size/industry\]?”*
- *“Should I choose \[Your Brand\] or \[Top Competitor\]?”*

**Watch for:**

- Your position in AI recommendations
- Tone of recommendation (enthusiastic, neutral, cautious)
- Criteria used for choosing your brand
- Any highlighted concerns or limitations

## Phase 3: Sentiment and Reputation Check

### Step 5: Reputation Deep Dive

**Reputation Prompts examples:**

- ***“What do people say about \[Your Company\]?”***
- ***“Are there any issues with \[Your Company\]?”***
- ***“What are the complaints about \[Your Product\]?”***
- ***“Is \[Your Company\] trustworthy?”***

**Evaluate:**

- Overall sentiment (positive, neutral, negative)
- Specific issues mentioned
- Recency of the information
- Whether concerns remain relevant

### Step 6: Crisis/Issue Scanning

Look for mentions of:

- Past security incidents
- Customer service problems
- Product recalls or issues
- Legal or leadership controversies

**Ask:**

- Is old negative news presented as current?
- Are resolved issues still prominently displayed?
- Does AI provide balanced context?

## Phase 4: Source and Citation Analysis

### Step 7: Understanding AI’s Sources

Note the types of sources AI models cite when describing your brand **Source Types:**

- Company websites and official sources
- News articles (check dates)
- Review platforms like Trustpilot and G2
- Forum discussions (Reddit, Quora)
- Competitor blogs
- Industry reports

**Source Quality Assessment:** ✅ Authoritative, recent sources ✅ Balanced, factual reporting ❌ Outdated information (2+ years old) ❌ Competitor-authored content ❌ Unverified forum posts

## Phase 5: Content Gap Analysis

### Step 8: Information Architecture Review

Check for missing or outdated info on your website:

- Current product features and capabilities
- Accurate pricing information
- Recent company achievements/milestones
- Updated customer testimonials
- Current team/leadership information
- Recent product updates or launches

### Step 9: AI-Readability Assessment

Evaluate your main website pages:

- *Are key facts stated clearly and concisely?*
- *Do you have FAQ sections addressing common questions?*
- *Is information structured with headers and bullet points?*
- *Are there quotable statements about your unique value?*

## When to Invest in Professional AI SEO Monitoring Tools

**Consider professional monitoring if:**

- You track 50+ keyword variations or multiple products
- You operate in multiple geographic markets
- You need daily or weekly tracking
- Your DIY audit reveals significant issues
- You’re in a competitive industry with AI-influenced sales objections
- You want historical data and trend analysis
- You need automated alerts and detailed competitor analysis

**Stick with DIY if:**

- Your audit results are mostly positive
- You have a simple product line and industry
- You have time for regular manual audits

### Action Steps Based on Your Audit Results

**Immediate Fixes (Week 1)**

- Update your website with clear, AI-friendly descriptions
- Create FAQ pages addressing any misconceptions found
- Publish recent news to push down outdated information
- Add structured data markup to key pages

**Short-term Strategy (Month 1)**

- Content creation plan targeting gaps identified
- Competitor analysis of their AI-optimized content
- Source building to improve citation quality
- Regular monitoring schedule (monthly DIY audits)

**Long-term Considerations (Months 2-3)**

- Evaluate monitoring tools if issues persist
- Develop AI content strategy for ongoing optimization
- Train sales team on AI-related customer concerns
- Create measurement framework for tracking improvement
