AIBrandScan Knowledge Hub is a practical resource library for teams that want to understand how their brand appears in AI-generated answers.

The hub covers AI search visibility, brand monitoring, competitor comparison, content structure, and practical workflows for improving how answer engines understand a product or company.

### What We Publish

- Guides for AI search visibility and brand monitoring
- Reusable prompts for audits, comparisons, and content analysis
- Use cases for marketing, founder-led growth, SEO, and product marketing teams
- Comparisons that clarify how AIBrandScan fits alongside adjacent tools

The goal is to make AI brand visibility concrete enough to measure, discuss, and improve.
