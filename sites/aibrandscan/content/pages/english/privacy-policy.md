#### Overview

This privacy policy explains how the AIBrandScan Knowledge Hub handles information connected with visits, messages, and site usage.

The hub is designed as a public content resource. You can read articles, browse prompts, and open public resources without creating an account on this site.

#### Information You Provide

If you contact AIBrandScan, we may receive the information you choose to send, such as your name, email address, message, company context, or collaboration request. This information is used to respond to your inquiry and manage the conversation.

#### Site Usage Information

Like most websites, the hosting and security infrastructure may process basic technical information such as IP address, browser type, requested URL, timestamps, and security events. This helps deliver the site, protect it from abuse, and understand whether pages are working correctly.

#### Cookies and Analytics

The Knowledge Hub does not require an account to browse. If analytics, embedded media, or third-party tools are enabled in the future, those providers may set their own cookies or process usage data according to their own policies.

#### Data Use

AIBrandScan uses submitted information only for the reason it was provided, such as replying to a message, discussing a project, or improving the clarity of the public resources.

#### Updates

This policy may be updated as the Knowledge Hub grows or as site features change. The latest version will remain available on this page.
