Use this prompt after client, consultant, or internal coordination meetings when notes need to become a practical task list.

## What it does

It extracts decisions, unresolved questions, dependencies, and next actions from raw meeting notes so the team can move from discussion to delivery.

## Best input

Paste the full meeting notes, add the project phase, and include any known deadlines or responsible team members.
